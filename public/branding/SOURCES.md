# Source marks

Source logos are used solely to identify the providers credited by 23 TV. They remain the marks of their respective owners. PNG wordmarks have had empty margins trimmed; their content, colors and aspect ratios are unchanged. CSS preserves aspect ratios within equal-size credit tiles.

- 60s API: project icon published by the official documentation at https://docs.60s-api.viki.moe — https://cdn.apifox.com/app/project-icon/custom/20250113/b7c54a22-10c8-4c76-b15e-b7b6f7290e81.png
- Douban Movie: https://asset.doubanio.com/cuphead/nav/2.0.0/movie/assets/lg_movie@2x.png (referenced by its official navigation stylesheet).
- Bangumi: https://bgm.tv/img/logo_rc1_2x.png (referenced by its official stylesheet).
- Baidu: https://www.baidu.com/favicon.ico, converted from the supplied 64px image to PNG.
- TMDB: existing approved original logo from https://www.themoviedb.org/about/logos-attribution, unchanged. Footer tooltip contains the official disclaimer; the TMDB recommendation section retains the original visible disclaimer as well.

The weekly film/TV lists are obtained through 60s API, with original Douban/Baidu data; Douban popular lists and Bangumi calendar are queried through their own respective endpoints.
